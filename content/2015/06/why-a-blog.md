Since I know about blogs I’ve always wanted to write one. Oh yes, I started some, but I wasn’t really comitted to none of them. As a consequence I let them all die silently.

I think it’s in my DNA showing others what I already know and new things I learn. My main goal is that everybody can get technically up-to-speed as fast as possible so they can do profitable stuff. And probably a blog is a very good starting point to achieve this goal, as it is like having an open window to the world.

And the question now is: given that you already had some blogs which silently died, why try it again? What is what has pushed me to create a domain name, set up a Wordpress and start to write stuff I don’t really know if anybody will ever read? The answer to that question is ‘thanks to an awesome guy who happens to have a [freely available course](https://t.dripemail2.com/c/eyJhY2NvdW50X2lkIjoiOTUyNDk2NiIsImRlbGl2ZXJ5X2lkIjoiMzMyMjU0NjUiLCJ1cmwiOiJodHRwOi8vZGV2Y2FyZWVyYm9vc3QuY29tL2Jsb2ctY291cnNlLz9fX3M9czVweDV6emllY3cxbXFmMzRlOGFcdTAwMjZ1dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249bGVzc29uLTUtZG8teW91LWtub3ctaG93LXRvLWdldC10cmFmZmljLWZvci15b3VyLWJsb2dcdTAwMjZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPWVtYWlsXHUwMDI2dXRtX3NvdXJjZT1ob3ctdG8tY3JlYXRlLWEtYmxvZy10aGF0LWJvb3N0cy15b3VyLWNhcmVlci1jb3Vyc2UifQ) on how to create your own blog, and, most important for me, **WHY you should have one**.

This guy is John Sonmez and he runs the site [SimpleProgrammer.com](https://t.dripemail2.com/c/eyJhY2NvdW50X2lkIjoiOTUyNDk2NiIsImRlbGl2ZXJ5X2lkIjoiMzMyMjU0NjUiLCJ1cmwiOiJodHRwOi8vc2ltcGxlcHJvZ3JhbW1lci5jb20_X19zPXM1cHg1enppZWN3MW1xZjM0ZThhXHUwMDI2dXRtX2NhbXBhaWduPWxlc3Nvbi01LWRvLXlvdS1rbm93LWhvdy10by1nZXQtdHJhZmZpYy1mb3IteW91ci1ibG9nXHUwMDI2dXRtX21lZGl1bT1lbWFpbFx1MDAyNnV0bV9zb3VyY2U9aG93LXRvLWNyZWF0ZS1hLWJsb2ctdGhhdC1ib29zdHMteW91ci1jYXJlZXItY291cnNlIn0). His site is full of deep insight about your career as a programmer. But he does not focus on teaching you how to program, you probably know how to do that (and if you don’t [check my blog for tips and tricks](https://www.easydevmixin.com/)!). His main focus is on ‘[soft skills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_skills)’. He writes about doubts that every developer has probably had on his career life, ranging from how to [crack your code interview](https://simpleprogrammer.com/2015/01/19/cracking-the-coding-interview/) to how to [best handle your finances](https://simpleprogrammer.com/2015/05/14/the-5-dollar-rule/). So, either if you are starting your career, or you are a senior developer you can take profit from his experience.
